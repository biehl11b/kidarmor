KidArmor Help

A private record of what happens at school, for parents.

Need help? Email info@coastlinecode.com and we'll get back to you. Please don't send your child's records. We don't need them to help, and we never store them.

Where are my records stored?

Only on your phone. There's no account and no server, so no one else can see them.

I'm getting a new phone. How do I move my records?

On your old phone, open the Child tab, go to Encrypted backup, choose a passphrase and save the backup to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email. On your new phone, install KidArmor, open the Child tab and choose Restore from a backup.

I forgot my PIN.

For your privacy, a forgotten PIN can't be reset by us. If Face ID or fingerprint is on, use that to get in, then change your PIN in the Child tab. Otherwise you'll need to delete and reinstall the app, then restore from a backup.

Recording doesn't start.

Make sure KidArmor is allowed to use the microphone: on iPhone, Settings → KidArmor → Microphone; on Android, Settings → Apps → KidArmor → Permissions. Always tell everyone in the meeting that you're recording. Some states require everyone's permission.

I'm not getting meeting reminders.

Turn on notifications for KidArmor in your phone's Settings. Meetings added to your calendar also remind you from your calendar app.

Is KidArmor legal advice?

No. KidArmor helps you organize facts. Laws and deadlines vary by state. For legal help, see the Resources tab.
