## Full description (both stores, 4,000 max)

KidArmor helps parents keep an organized, private record of what happens with their child at school, so when it's time to speak up, you have the facts.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

KEEP THE RECORD
• Log bullying, IEP and 504 problems, calls with the school and meetings, each with its own category
• Add photos and documents to any entry
• Talk instead of type
• Save school emails without copying and pasting
• Write or say a note when you're upset. KidArmor keeps your words private and makes a calm, factual version you can send
• See unanswered reports, overdue follow-ups and accommodations that keep getting missed

RECORD MEETINGS
• Record school meetings with a reminder to tell everyone it's being recorded
• Live transcript while the meeting happens
• When you stop, KidArmor saves a summary: problems raised, what the school agreed to, next steps and the next meeting, with a tab for the full transcript
• Keep recording while you check notes in the app
• If it's quiet for 5 minutes, KidArmor asks if the meeting is over

STAY ON TOP OF MEETINGS
• Schedule meetings with two reminders you choose
• Add them to your phone's calendar
• A Record button appears when it's time

REPORTS FOR DOCTORS AND COUNSELORS
• Keep dated report cards, progress reports and test scores
• Optional medication and treatment notes
• One tap makes a PDF in date order for a doctor, psychiatrist or counselor

LETTERS AND SHARING
• Letters and email summaries filled in from your record
• A PDF case binder with entries, photos, documents and transcripts
• Open your case on a computer with a locked link and code
• Share a read-only copy with an advocate (your private notes stay private)
• Teachers can send you updates with a link, no app needed

RESOURCES
• Mississippi parent centers and advocacy groups with tap-to-call and website buttons, and help for parents in every state

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Lock with a PIN and Face ID or fingerprint
• Encrypted backups you control, to move to a new phone
• Up to 5 children

KidArmor helps you organize facts. It isn't legal advice. Recording laws vary by state; always tell everyone when you're recording.

## Apple: App Privacy ("nutrition label")

Choose **Data Not Collected**. KidArmor has no server, no analytics and no ads; everything stays on the device.

## Apple: Age rating questionnaire

Answer **None** to every content question. Unrestricted web access: **No**. Result: **4+**.
(The app mentions bullying only as something parents record; it has no violent or mature content.)

## Apple: Notes for the reviewer (App Review Information)

KidArmor needs no login. On first open, enter any parent name, email and state, add a child, and set any 4-digit PIN.
To try the main features: tap Record a meeting, then Start recording, speak for a few seconds and tap Stop recording. A summary and transcript are saved automatically. Tap Schedule a meeting to add a meeting with two reminders. All data is stored only on the device; there is no account or server.
Demo account: not needed.

## Apple: Export compliance

The app uses only standard encryption (AES, built into the system) to protect the user's own backups, so it qualifies for the exemption. Info.plist already sets ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO.
