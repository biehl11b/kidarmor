## Full description (both stores, 4,000 max)

KidArmor helps parents keep an organized, private record of what happens with their child at school, so when it's time to speak up, you have the facts.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no tracking and no pop-up ads.

KEEP THE RECORD
• Log bullying, IEP and 504 problems, calls with the school and meetings, each with its own category
• Add photos and documents to any entry
• Talk instead of type
• Save school emails without copying and pasting
• Write or say a note when you're upset. KidArmor keeps your words private and makes a calm, factual version you can send
• See unanswered reports, overdue follow-ups and accommodations that keep getting missed

RECORD MEETINGS
• Record school meetings with a reminder to tell everyone it's being recorded
• Live transcript while the meeting happens
• When you stop, KidArmor saves a summary: problems raised, what the school agreed to, next steps and the next meeting, with a tab for the full transcript
• Keep recording while you check notes in the app
• If it's quiet for 5 minutes, KidArmor asks if the meeting is over

STAY ON TOP OF MEETINGS
• Schedule meetings with two reminders you choose
• Add them to your phone's calendar
• Get ready for each meeting: what's happened since the last one, your goals, your concerns in your own words, questions built from your records and a printable meeting plan
• A Record button appears when it's time

DEADLINES
• When you ask for school records or an evaluation, KidArmor tracks the deadline under federal law and reminds you before it's due
• Links to your state's own special education office for its timelines

REPORTS FOR DOCTORS AND COUNSELORS
• Keep dated report cards, progress reports and test scores
• Optional medication and treatment notes
• One tap makes a PDF in date order for a doctor, psychiatrist or counselor

LETTERS AND SHARING
• Letters and email summaries filled in from your record: report bullying, ask for a safety plan, the plan isn't being followed, ask for an evaluation or records, meeting recaps and follow-ups when the school hasn't answered
• Choose which entries go into each letter
• A PDF case binder with entries, photos, documents and transcripts
• Open your case on a computer with a locked link and code
• Share with your attorney, advocate, counselor, doctor or tutor: you choose which parts they see, and send updates later (your private notes stay private)
• Teachers can send you updates with a link, no app needed
• Share with the other parent: pair your phones once with a QR code, then send each other what you add. Nothing goes through a server

RESOURCES
• Your state's parent center and protection and advocacy agency, with tap-to-call and website buttons, in every state
• Attorneys, advocates, evaluators, counselors, therapists, doctors and tutors in your state, clearly labeled as paid listings (KidArmor doesn't recommend or refer anyone)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Lock with a PIN and Face ID or fingerprint
• Encrypted backups you control, to move to a new phone
• Up to 5 children

EASY TO USE
• Works with VoiceOver and TalkBack
• Larger text: follows your iPhone's text size, or choose a size in the app
• Respects Reduce Motion
• See a made-up example family before you start

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
• Email, call or text us from the app. We never see your records.

KidArmor helps you organize facts. It isn't legal advice. Recording laws vary by state; always tell everyone when you're recording.

## Apple: Notes for the reviewer (App Review Information)

KidArmor needs no login, account or server. On first open, tick "I have read and agree" and tap Continue. Setup screen 1 offers language and bigger text (optional), then enter any parent name, email and state, add a child, and set any 4-digit PIN (for example 1234). If the app later says "KidArmor is locked", type that PIN.
To see the app filled in without typing, tap "See an example family first" during setup.
To try the main features: tap Record a meeting. The first time, KidArmor asks how to make transcripts (on this phone only, or Apple's speech service); either choice works. Tap Start recording, speak for a few seconds and tap Stop recording. A summary and transcript are saved automatically. On an upcoming meeting, "Get ready" makes a one-page meeting prep sheet. The Letters tab fills in letters from the record. Deadlines (Plan tab) shows federal IEP/504 timelines with reminders.
Sharing with the other parent (Child tab, optional) needs a second iPhone: phone 1 shows a QR code, phone 2 scans it with the Camera app, then the parents send each other a locked file through Messages, Mail or AirDrop. Nothing goes through a server. Everything else works on one phone.
"Share with my professional" makes a locked link and code that a parent sends to their own attorney or counselor; nothing is uploaded to us.
Name and email are asked for only to fill in the parent's own letters and emails; they stay on the phone and are never sent to us.
Features that use the phone itself: microphone recording with on-device or Apple speech transcription, Face ID / passcode lock, local notifications for meeting and deadline reminders, the system "New Event" calendar screen, the camera for photos of school documents, and the share sheet for letters, PDFs, co-parent files and encrypted backups.
Advertising: the Resources tab can show local professionals (for example, special-education attorneys) who pay to be listed, each labeled "Paid listing". There may be none listed yet. There are no ad networks, no tracking and no pop-up ads.
Demo account: not needed.
