Tips that help in any school dispute
  • Put requests in writing and keep a copy. KidArmor's Letters tab does this for you.
  • After a meeting or phone call, send a short recap email of what was agreed.
  • Ask for copies of documents before a meeting, so you have time to read them.
  • You can ask for time to review a document before you sign it.
  • You can bring someone with you to meetings, such as a spouse, friend or advocate.
  • Write things down the same day, with dates, names and exact words when you can.

These tips and the links above are for information. They aren't legal advice; rights and deadlines vary by state.

Child & privacy

Use KidArmor on a computer

Read everything on a bigger screen, and write letters, send emails and make the PDF binder from your computer.

  1. Tap Open on my computer. KidArmor makes a locked link and a code.
  2. Text or email the link to yourself, open it on your computer, and type the code.

The link carries your written record. Recordings, photos and documents stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Your children

Each child has their own case file, plan and documents.

Where your information lives

  • Entries, photos and documents are saved on this phone only.
  • Nothing is uploaded to a server. There's no account to hack.
  • Information leaves only when you send a letter, save a file, or make a backup.
Space used on this phone

Because nothing is stored online, clearing this browser's data erases the record. Make an encrypted backup below.

Permissions

Lock

Set a PIN so no one else can open your records on this device. The phone app uses Face ID or fingerprint.

Face ID or fingerprint

Encrypted backup

Saves one locked file with every case, photo, document, recording and report card. Keep it in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email. Use it to move to a new phone. Without your passphrase nobody can open it, including us.

Write the passphrase down. If it's forgotten, the backup can't be opened.

Share with an advocate

Your advocate or lawyer gets a read-only copy of this child's case by a locked link. Send the code separately, by phone or a different text. Private notes are never shared. Recordings, photos and documents stay on your phone.
