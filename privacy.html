KidArmor Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · KidArmor is made by Coastline Code LLC, Mississippi

The short version: KidArmor doesn't collect your information. Everything you put in the app is stored only on your own phone. There's no account, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Information leaves your phone only when you choose to send, share or save it.

What KidArmor stores, and where

The records you create are saved only on your device. That includes your name and contact details, your child's name and school, entries, notes, photos, documents, meeting recordings, transcripts, report cards and treatment notes. Coastline Code LLC does not run a server that receives or stores them, and we cannot see, access or recover them.

Information we collect

None. KidArmor does not collect personal information, usage data, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or analytics. It contains no advertising and no third-party tracking tools.

When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to do it:

  • Emails and letters you send open in your own email app.
  • PDFs (case binder, doctor report) and backups are saved or shared where you pick, using your phone's share sheet.
  • Calendar: meetings you schedule are added to your phone's calendar, if you allow it.
  • Links you make to open your case on a computer or share it with an advocate carry an encrypted copy of your written record inside the link itself. They're locked with a code only you see. The link is not stored by us.
  • Teacher updates: when a teacher sends you an update link, the message travels inside that link. KidArmor asks you before adding it.
  • Websites of organizations in the Resources tab open in your browser when you tap them, and their own privacy policies apply.

Phone permissions and why we ask

  • Microphone: to record meetings and let you talk instead of type.
  • Speech recognition: to write a transcript of recordings and spoken notes. Depending on your phone and settings, your phone's speech service (Apple or Google) may process the audio to create the text, under their privacy policies.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: to unlock the app. Your face or fingerprint never leaves your phone and is never seen by KidArmor.
  • Notifications: meeting reminders.
  • Calendar: to add meetings you schedule.
  • Camera and photos: to add pictures of documents and report cards.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's Settings at any time.

Security

KidArmor can be locked with a PIN and Face ID or fingerprint. Backups and shared links are encrypted (AES-256) with a passphrase or code you control. Keep your phone's own lock turned on, too.

Children

KidArmor is meant for parents and guardians, not for children to use. Parents enter information about their own child, and it stays on the parent's phone.

Deleting your information

Use Erase everything on this phone in the Child tab, or delete the app. Because we never receive your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Backups or files you saved elsewhere must be deleted where you saved them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
