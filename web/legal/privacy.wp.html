Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use KidArmor, the app made by Coastline Code LLC ("we," "us"). The short version: what you put in KidArmor stays on your phone. We don't collect it, see it, sell it or share it.

1. What we don't collect

KidArmor doesn't require an account. We don't collect or receive your name, email, phone number or address; anything you type into KidArmor, including notes, logs, contacts and your child's information; your photos, documents or meeting recordings; or your location, contacts list or browsing history. KidArmor has no ads, no ad tracking and no tracking across other apps or websites.

2. Where your information is stored

Everything you save in KidArmor is stored on your device. Meeting summaries are made on your device. Your recordings aren't sent to us or to an outside AI service. On Android, KidArmor's records are left out of Google cloud backups and phone-to-phone transfers. On iPhone, if your phone backs up to iCloud, your iPhone's backup settings control whether KidArmor's data is included; those backups are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy, and we can't see them. KidArmor also lets you make your own encrypted backup file (on the Child tab), locked with a passphrase only you know. Because we never receive your information, we can't recover it if you lose your phone or delete the app, so keep a backup or a PDF copy.

The one exception: live transcripts. While you record a meeting, KidArmor turns what's said into text using your phone's built-in speech recognition from Apple (on iPhone) or Google (on Android). On some phones, Apple or Google may process that audio on their own servers to make the text, under their own privacy policies. You can choose instead to have transcripts made on your phone only (Child tab → Transcripts); some phones can't do that, and then KidArmor records without a live transcript. The recording and the transcript are saved only on your phone, and we never receive them. Everything else you save stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

3. Information you choose to share

  • PDFs and exports: when you share a PDF of your record, you decide who gets it and how it's sent. After that, it's handled by the app you used to send it, such as email or text messages.
  • Opening your case on a computer: when you use the locked link and short code, your written record goes from your phone to your own computer. It isn't stored by us.
  • Sharing with a professional: when you share with an attorney, advocate, counselor, doctor or tutor, you choose which parts of your record to send. They go in a locked link that only opens with the code you give them, and they aren't stored on our servers. Private notes are never included. Once the professional opens the copy, it's on their device and handled under their own rules.
  • Teacher updates: when a teacher sends you an update, the message travels inside the link. It isn't stored online by us. You choose whether to add it to your record.
  • Emailing or calling us: if you contact us, we receive what you send, such as your email address and message. We use it only to answer you and to improve KidArmor. Please don't send us private details about your child or your case.

4. Parent resources and paid listings

KidArmor shows parent help organizations and paid listings from attorneys, advocates, evaluators, counselors, therapists, doctors and tutors for your state. Paid listings are clearly labeled. To keep these lists current, the app may download the latest version from our server. That download doesn't include any of your information, and it's the same list for everyone. Like any internet request, the website that hosts the list can see your device's internet (IP) address and may keep it briefly in standard server logs; we don't use it to identify you or connect it to anything in the app. We don't track which listings you look at or tap. If you tap a listing's website, phone number or email, that business's own privacy policy applies to anything you share with them.

5. App stores

Apple and Google may collect information when you download or use apps from their stores, such as crash reports or download counts, under their own privacy policies. Any reports they share with us are summaries that don't identify you.

6. Children

KidArmor is made for parents and guardians, not for children to use. Parents may enter information about their own children. That information stays on the parent's device and is never sent to us.

7. Deleting your information

You can delete any entry in the app, or delete everything by deleting the app. Since we don't have your information, there's nothing for us to delete on our end. If you emailed us and want that email deleted, ask us at the address below.

8. Security

Your information is protected by your phone's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID or fingerprint, and by KidArmor's own lock if you turn it on. Keep your phone locked and your software up to date.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
