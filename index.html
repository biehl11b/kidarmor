Tips that help in any school dispute
  • Put requests in writing and keep a copy. KidArmor's Letters tab does this for you.
  • After a meeting or phone call, send a short recap email of what was agreed.
  • Ask for copies of documents before a meeting, so you have time to read them.
  • You can ask for time to review a document before you sign it.
  • You can bring someone with you to meetings, such as a spouse, friend or advocate.
  • Write things down the same day, with dates, names and exact words when you can.

These tips and the links above are for information. They aren't legal advice; rights and deadlines vary by state.
